Feeding sheet

The plan's weekly feeding: on each machine one chamber is fed by its card, the other cures with no food at all. Weekdays only. Log every feed on Quick score with the same band and wetness; never enter the card anywhere.

Loading…

How much: the ranges are the feed form's bands: small under 250 g, medium 250–500 g, large over 500 g. A binge or holiday card's big feed is 700–900 g. Food and any water together count (1 mL of water = 1 g). Wet means mostly watery food (vegetables, fruit, cooked leftovers) or water added; normal means mixed kitchen waste.

Record each feed on Quick score → Feed: the chamber, the load band (small / medium / large), the wetness (dry / normal / wet), and the grams if you weighed it. Pick the band of what you actually fed.

Times are suggestions between the morning score and the 15:00 mix, so no score finds fresh food. Close to the time is fine.

Made by python -m harness.tools.feed_schedule from a fixed seed, as the plan asks. Print with Ctrl+P: one week per sheet.
